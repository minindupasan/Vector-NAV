VECTOR NAV — Warehouse Location Directory

Loading Bay
The Loading Bay is located at the north end of the warehouse. It is used for receiving and dispatching goods.
It is marked with AprilTag ID 1. Coordinates: x=1.5, y=0.5. The loading bay has a large roller door and a weighing scale.
Staff must wear high-visibility vests in the loading bay. The area fits up to two forklifts simultaneously.

Isle 1
Isle 1 is the first storage aisle on the left side of the warehouse. It stores fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG).
It is marked with AprilTag ID 2. Coordinates: x=3.0, y=1.2. Isle 1 contains shelving units A through F.
Products here include packaged food, beverages, and household items. Isle 1 is restocked every Monday and Thursday.

Isle 2
Isle 2 is the second storage aisle, adjacent to Isle 1. It stores medium-weight components and spare parts.
It is marked with AprilTag ID 3. Coordinates: x=3.0, y=2.8. Isle 2 contains shelving units G through L.
Heavy lifting equipment is available at the end of Isle 2.

Isle 3
Isle 3 is the third storage aisle at the far right of the warehouse. It stores bulk and oversized items.
It is marked with AprilTag ID 4. Coordinates: x=3.0, y=4.4. Isle 3 has reinforced flooring rated for 500kg loads.

Charging Station
The Charging Station is located near the main entrance at the south wall.
It is marked with AprilTag ID 5. Coordinates: x=0.1, y=0.1. The robot should return here when battery is below 20%.
The charging station has two docking ports. Average full charge time is 2 hours.

Control Room
The Control Room is the operations hub located at the east side of the warehouse.
It is marked with AprilTag ID 6. Coordinates: x=5.0, y=2.5. The control room contains monitoring systems and the main computer terminal.
Only authorized personnel are allowed in the control room.

Break Room
The Break Room is located at the south-east corner of the warehouse.
It is marked with AprilTag ID 7. Coordinates: x=5.0, y=0.5. The break room has seating for 10 staff members.
It contains a kitchen area, vending machines, and a first aid kit.

Emergency Exit
The Emergency Exit is located at the west wall of the warehouse.
It is marked with AprilTag ID 8. Coordinates: x=0.0, y=3.0. The emergency exit leads to the muster point in the car park.
This exit must remain clear at all times and must not be blocked by the robot or any goods.
